# MockupFX Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative community. Participation in MockupFX is expected to be free from harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and personal attacks.

## Expected behavior

Use respectful language, assume good faith while asking clarifying questions, focus criticism on ideas and implementations rather than people, give credit for contributions, and consider the impact of decisions on users with different accessibility, privacy, and operational needs.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment; hate speech; sexualized language or imagery; threats; stalking; doxxing; sustained disruption; personal insults; publishing private information without permission; knowingly sharing secrets or malicious code; and any conduct that would reasonably make collaboration unsafe or hostile.

## Enforcement

Reports may be made privately through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) when that is appropriate, or through the project's designated private maintainer contact once published. Maintainers will acknowledge reports, assess the available information, protect confidentiality where possible, and may issue a warning, remove content, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant. Serious security or safety reports will be handled with heightened confidentiality.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, chat spaces, events, and other project-associated public or private interactions.

## Attribution

This policy is adapted in spirit from common open-source community standards. It may evolve as the project develops.
