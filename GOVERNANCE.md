# MockupFX Governance

## Purpose

MockupFX is an open-source project that aims to keep interactive prototype work portable, inspectable, and self-hostable. Governance exists to protect that goal, to make technical decisions visible, and to ensure that contributors can participate without needing private access to a vendor-controlled roadmap.

## Roles

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Contributors** | Propose, discuss, document, test, review, and implement changes under the project policies |
| **Reviewers** | Review changes in areas where they have demonstrated expertise; identify risks and request evidence |
| **Maintainers** | Merge pull requests, triage issues, protect releases, and uphold the roadmap, security policy, and code of conduct |
| **Release maintainers** | Prepare versioned releases, changelogs, migration notes, artifact integrity information, and disclosure coordination |
| **Project steward** | Resolves a documented deadlock, appoints maintainers, and ensures the project remains aligned with its stated mission |

## Decision-making

Routine fixes, documentation corrections, test improvements, and scoped implementations can be approved by one maintainer after appropriate review. Changes to public schemas, compatibility policies, interaction execution rules, sharing/access policy, authorization roles, encryption/key handling, telemetry policy, or release governance require a written proposal and approval by at least two maintainers where the team size permits.

A proposal must state the problem, users affected, alternatives considered, compatibility/migration plan, security and privacy implications, accessibility impact, operational impact, and acceptance evidence. Decisions are made in public issues, discussions, or architecture decision records. If consensus cannot be reached, the project steward records the decision, rationale, dissenting concerns, and a reevaluation trigger.

## Maintainer expectations

Maintainers must act in the project interest, apply the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) consistently, avoid merging their own substantial change without independent review where practical, disclose conflicts of interest, and protect secrets and release credentials. Repeated inactivity, policy violations, or loss of trust may lead to a role change by documented maintainer decision.

## Transparency and forks

MockupFX's public format, documentation, and release process are intended to be auditable. Contributors are encouraged to fork, adapt, self-host, and develop compatible implementations consistent with the license. A fork may use a different governance or hosting model, but it should clearly identify itself and avoid confusion about official compatibility or security support.

## Amendments

Governance changes require a public proposal, at least seven days for comment unless an urgent security or safety issue requires faster action, and recorded maintainer approval. The project steward may make a temporary emergency decision, which must be documented and reviewed afterward.
